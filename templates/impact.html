Impact Areas

Job Creation & Employment

We create direct and indirect employment opportunities through the enterprises we build. From manufacturing roles to service delivery positions, each venture generates decent work for youth, women, and vulnerable populations. Our training programs equip beneficiaries with practical skills that increase their employability and earning potential.

Productivity Improvement

Our technologies and innovations are designed to increase productivity across key sectors. From agricultural equipment that reduces labor and post-harvest losses to digital tools that streamline business operations, we help individuals and enterprises achieve more with less.

Environmental Sustainability

Every technology and enterprise we develop considers environmental impact. We prioritize green technologies, renewable energy, circular economy principles, and climate-resilient solutions that protect natural resources while creating economic value.

Inclusive Economic Growth

We intentionally design programs and enterprises to include underserved and vulnerable groups. By creating opportunities for women, youth, refugees, persons with disabilities, and rural communities, we contribute to more equitable and inclusive economic development across Ethiopia.

Success Stories

Training & Skills

432 Youth Trained in Green Technologies

Through 45 training batches, we equipped young Ethiopians with practical skills in renewable energy, sustainable manufacturing, and digital innovation — creating a pipeline of skilled workers for the green economy.

Enterprise Building

35 Sustainable Enterprises Launched

From agro-processing cooperatives to renewable energy startups, our ventures are creating jobs, generating income, and solving real community challenges across 15+ communities.

Community Impact

1,200+ Jobs Created Across Ethiopia

Our Innovation-to-Livelihood approach has generated over 1,200 direct and indirect jobs, with 233 trainees successfully connected to employers and enterprise opportunities.
