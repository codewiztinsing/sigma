About Sigma

Discover why we were established, what makes us different, and how our Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Studio model transforms innovation into sustainable livelihoods across Ethiopia.

Why Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation Was Established

In Ethiopia, many talented innovators, entrepreneurs, and aspiring changemakers never realize their ideas—not because they lack creativity, technical skills, or determination, but because they encounter systemic barriers long before their innovations can become sustainable enterprises. Limited access to finance, business development support, manufacturing facilities, markets, strategic partnerships, mentorship, and commercialization pathways often prevents promising ideas from progressing beyond the concept stage.

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation was established by founders who personally experienced these challenges while developing innovative technologies and participating in an incubation program at Addis Ababa University. Their firsthand experiences exposed critical gaps in Ethiopia's innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystem, where support is often available only after a startup has already formed, secured resources, or demonstrated significant progress.

These experiences inspired a different vision. Rather than creating another traditional incubator, accelerator, or venture studio, the founders established Sigma as a Social Innovation Ecosystem Builder—an organization that connects every element required for innovation to succeed. Sigma brings together innovation, entrepreneurship, technology development, finance, manufacturing, capacity building, market access, strategic partnerships, and ecosystem collaboration to help transform ideas into sustainable enterprises and lasting livelihood opportunities.

By proactively identifying opportunities, removing barriers, mobilizing resources, and building inclusive innovation ecosystems, Sigma empowers innovators, entrepreneurs, youth, women, unemployed graduates, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other economically vulnerable groups to realize their potential. Its ultimate mission is not only to support individual ventures but also to create decent jobs, strengthen local industries, reduce poverty, and contribute to Ethiopia's inclusive and sustainable economic development.

The Challenges We Experienced

Our journey exposed structural obstacles that prevent many Ethiopian innovators from progressing beyond the idea stage. These include:

Limited access to affordable prototyping, fabrication, and manufacturing facilities
Shortage of engineering components, testing equipment, and specialized materials
High costs of importing research and development materials and equipment
Limited financing for prototype development and technology validation
Bureaucratic administrative procedures and fragmented institutional support
Premature business registration requirements before ideas are commercially ready
Monthly tax reporting obligations despite having no business revenue
Limited access to innovation workspaces, laboratories, and fabrication facilities
Weak coordination among institutions responsible for supporting innovation
Limited commercialization support for locally developed technologies

These barriers discourage innovators long before investors, incubators, or accelerators become involved.

Why Sigma Is Different

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation was not established to compete with traditional incubators, accelerators, or venture studios. Instead, it was created to address the systemic barriers that prevent innovative ideas from becoming sustainable enterprises and to build the ecosystems needed for innovation to thrive.

While many support organizations begin working with entrepreneurs after a startup has already been formed, Sigma starts much earlier. It identifies pressing livelihood challenges and untapped market opportunities, develops practical technologies and enterprise solutions, validates business models, mobilizes finance and partnerships, strengthens technical and entrepreneurial capacity, facilitates manufacturing and commercialization, and connects innovators with the resources required to succeed.

Rather than supporting isolated startups, Sigma builds integrated innovation ecosystems where innovators, entrepreneurs, investors, manufacturers, research institutions, government, development partners, and communities collaborate to transform ideas into sustainable enterprises that create decent jobs and lasting social impact.

For Sigma, an enterprise is not the starting point—it is the result of a well-functioning innovation ecosystem. By connecting innovation, entrepreneurship, technology, finance, manufacturing, market access, and strategic partnerships, Sigma enables economically vulnerable groups to realize their potential, build resilient livelihoods, strengthen local industries, and contribute to Ethiopia's inclusive and sustainable economic development.

How Sigma Reduces Innovation Risks

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation recognizes that transforming an idea into a sustainable enterprise is a complex journey involving technical, financial, institutional, regulatory, manufacturing, and market-related risks. No single organization can effectively address all of these challenges alone. For this reason, Sigma serves as a Social Innovation Ecosystem Builder and Orchestrator, bringing together the right people, institutions, resources, and opportunities to reduce the risks that innovators and entrepreneurs should not have to face alone.

Rather than attempting to own every facility or service, Sigma builds collaborative innovation ecosystems by mobilizing the strengths of strategic partners and connecting innovators with the support they need at every stage of their journey. Depending on the needs of each innovation, Sigma facilitates access to:

  • Shared innovation, prototyping, testing, and fabrication facilities
  • Engineering design, research, technology development, and product improvement expertise
  • Manufacturing technologies, production facilities, and standard engineering components
  • Technical mentorship, entrepreneurship development, and innovation management support
  • Crowdfunding, investment opportunities, financial institutions, and enterprise financing mechanisms
  • Regulatory guidance, intellectual property support, standards compliance, and administrative facilitation
  • Government agencies, universities, TVET institutions, research centers, manufacturers, private sector partners, investors, and development organizations
  • Market research, pilot implementation, customer validation, commercialization support, and market access
  • Venture creation, enterprise development, incubation, acceleration, and business scaling services once innovations demonstrate technical, commercial, and social viability

By integrating these capabilities into a coordinated ecosystem, Sigma enables innovators to focus on solving real-world challenges while significantly reducing technical, institutional, financial, manufacturing, and commercialization risks. This collaborative approach accelerates the transformation of innovative ideas into sustainable enterprises, creates decent jobs, strengthens local industries, and expands opportunities for youth, women, unemployed graduates, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other economically vulnerable groups to build lasting livelihoods.

At Sigma, innovation succeeds not because one organization does everything, but because a well-connected ecosystem ensures that every innovator has access to the knowledge, partnerships, resources, and opportunities needed to realize their ideas and create meaningful impact.

Sigma's Innovation-to-Livelihood Ecosystem Model (ILEM)

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation pioneers the Innovation-to-Livelihood Ecosystem Model (ILEM)—an integrated social innovation approach that transforms ideas into sustainable enterprises, decent jobs, and lasting livelihood opportunities through ecosystem collaboration.

Unlike traditional incubators, accelerators, and venture studios, which primarily support entrepreneurs after startups have already been established, Sigma begins much earlier. It proactively identifies community livelihood challenges and untapped market opportunities, develops practical innovations and technologies, validates solutions in real-world settings, mobilizes finance and strategic partnerships, facilitates manufacturing and commercialization, builds sustainable enterprises, and strengthens the ecosystem needed for long-term growth.

Rather than supporting isolated ventures, Sigma connects innovation, entrepreneurship, technology, finance, manufacturing, market access, capacity building, research institutions, government, private sector partners, investors, and communities into a coordinated ecosystem where innovation can thrive and create measurable social, economic, and environmental impact.

Through this ecosystem-driven approach, Sigma empowers innovators, entrepreneurs, youth, women, unemployed graduates, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other economically vulnerable groups to realize their ideas, create decent jobs, strengthen local industries, and contribute to Ethiopia's inclusive and sustainable economic development.

Who We Are

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation is an Ethiopian Social Innovation Ecosystem Builder dedicated to transforming ideas into sustainable enterprises and opportunities into lasting livelihoods.

We build and orchestrate innovation ecosystems that connect innovation, entrepreneurship, technology development, finance, manufacturing, market access, capacity building, and strategic partnerships to enable innovators and entrepreneurs to transform their ideas into enterprises that create decent jobs and generate lasting social, economic, and environmental impact.

Rather than waiting for startups to emerge, Sigma proactively identifies community livelihood challenges and untapped market opportunities, develops practical technologies and innovative solutions, validates them in real-world settings, mobilizes ecosystem partners and resources, supports commercialization and enterprise creation, and strengthens the ecosystems required for long-term growth and sustainability.

By bringing together government, universities, TVET institutions, research organizations, manufacturers, investors, development partners, the private sector, and local communities, Sigma creates collaborative ecosystems where innovation can flourish and solutions can be translated into scalable enterprises and inclusive economic opportunities.

Our mission is to empower innovators, entrepreneurs, youth, women, unemployed graduates, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other economically vulnerable groups to realize their dreams, build resilient enterprises, create decent jobs, and contribute to Ethiopia's inclusive and sustainable economic development.

Sigma does not simply support startups—we build the ecosystems that enable innovation to become enterprise, enterprise to create livelihoods, and livelihoods to transform communities.

Our Mission

To build inclusive innovation ecosystems that transform ideas into sustainable enterprises, create decent jobs, strengthen local industries, and empower economically vulnerable people to achieve lasting livelihoods through innovation, entrepreneurship, technology, and strategic partnerships.

We achieve this through four strategic missions:

1

Build and Orchestrate Inclusive Innovation Ecosystems

Create and strengthen collaborative ecosystems that connect innovators, entrepreneurs, communities, government, universities, TVET institutions, research organizations, manufacturers, investors, financial institutions, development partners, and the private sector. By mobilizing knowledge, resources, infrastructure, finance, and strategic partnerships, Sigma creates enabling environments where innovation can flourish and sustainable enterprises can emerge.

2

De-risk Innovation and Empower Innovators

Support innovators, inventors, researchers, engineers, technopreneurs, and communities whose ideas are still too early for traditional incubators, accelerators, investors, or commercial markets. Sigma reduces technical, financial, institutional, manufacturing, regulatory, and commercialization risks by facilitating access to research and development, engineering expertise, prototyping, testing, mentorship, finance, manufacturing capabilities, market validation, commercialization pathways, and ecosystem partners—enabling promising ideas to become sustainable enterprises.

3

Transform Challenges into Sustainable Enterprises and Livelihoods

Identify pressing livelihood challenges and untapped market opportunities, then develop practical technologies, products, services, and enterprise models that address real-world needs. Sigma validates innovative solutions, supports commercialization, and establishes sustainable enterprises that create decent jobs, improve productivity, strengthen local industries, promote environmentally responsible development, and expand livelihood opportunities for youth, women, unemployed graduates, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other economically vulnerable groups.

4

Scale Innovation for Inclusive Economic Development

Replicate successful innovations, enterprise models, and ecosystem partnerships to maximize long-term impact across sectors and regions. By strengthening local innovation capacity, facilitating technology transfer, expanding market access, and fostering cross-sector collaboration, Sigma accelerates the growth of resilient enterprises, creates sustainable employment, and contributes to Ethiopia's inclusive, competitive, and sustainable economic development.

Rather than asking, "How do we build more startups?"
Sigma asks, "How do we build the ecosystems that enable innovators to solve livelihood challenges, transform their ideas into sustainable enterprises, and create decent jobs that improve people's lives?"

This philosophy defines Sigma's Innovation-to-Livelihood Ecosystem Model.

Innovation is the catalyst—not the destination.
Our ultimate goal is to build ecosystems that transform ideas into sustainable enterprises, create decent jobs, improve quality of life, and drive inclusive economic development.

Who We Serve

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation exists to ensure that talent is not limited by opportunity. We build inclusive innovation ecosystems that enable individuals and communities to transform ideas into sustainable enterprises, create decent jobs, and achieve lasting livelihoods.

We recognize that economic vulnerability is not defined solely by income. It also includes people with talent, creativity, technical skills, education, or entrepreneurial ambition who lack access to finance, technology, manufacturing facilities, mentorship, markets, partnerships, or the institutional support needed to realize their potential.

Our Primary Communities

Innovators, inventors, researchers, engineers, and technopreneurs developing solutions to real-world challenges
Youth and women seeking opportunities for entrepreneurship, employment, and economic empowerment
Unemployed TVET and university graduates, particularly engineering and technology graduates, who possess valuable skills but face barriers to productive employment and enterprise creation
Refugees, internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities striving to rebuild resilient and sustainable livelihoods
Persons with disabilities seeking accessible technologies, inclusive workplaces, entrepreneurship opportunities, and dignified employment
Smallholder farmers and rural communities requiring affordable technologies, climate-smart innovations, value-added processing, and improved market access to increase productivity and incomes
Micro, small, and growing enterprises seeking innovation, technology upgrading, manufacturing support, commercialization, and business growth opportunities
Informal sector workers and aspiring entrepreneurs transitioning toward productive, resilient, and sustainable enterprises
Low-income households and underserved communities that need affordable products, services, and technologies that improve quality of life and expand economic opportunity

Sigma also works closely with government institutions, universities, TVET institutions, research organizations, manufacturers, investors, financial institutions, development partners, private-sector organizations, cooperatives, and civil society to build collaborative ecosystems that enable innovation and inclusive economic development.

Every innovation, enterprise, partnership, and program we develop is intentionally designed to be inclusive. Whether creating physical technologies, digital platforms, manufacturing enterprises, or ecosystem initiatives, Sigma ensures that underserved populations have equitable access to innovation, technical skills, finance, production facilities, strategic partnerships, commercialization support, and market opportunities.

Unlike conventional innovation organizations that primarily support existing startups, Sigma proactively identifies untapped potential, removes systemic barriers, mobilizes ecosystem resources, and creates opportunities where they do not yet exist. Our goal is not simply to help people start businesses, but to empower them to become innovators, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, manufacturers, employers, and leaders who contribute to resilient local industries and inclusive economic growth.

By connecting people with the ecosystems they need to succeed, Sigma transforms ideas into sustainable enterprises, enterprises into decent jobs, and jobs into lasting livelihoods that strengthen families, communities, and Ethiopia's future.
