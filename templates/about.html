About Shedex

Discover why we were established, what makes us different, and how our Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Studio model transforms innovation into sustainable livelihoods across Ethiopia.

Shedex's Approach

Shedex was not established to compete with traditional incubators, accelerators, or other venture-support organizations. It was created to address a different gap: the barriers that prevent practical solutions from becoming viable ventures and prevent economically vulnerable people from accessing meaningful opportunities for livelihoods and employment.

While many support organizations begin working with entrepreneurs after a startup or business already exists, Shedex starts much earlier. It identifies pressing social and economic challenges, unmet needs, and untapped opportunities; develops practical and affordable solutions; builds venture models around those solutions; and tests them through real-world pilots.

Shedex follows a build, pilot, and handover approach. Rather than simply providing training, advice, or resources to people who are expected to build their own ventures, Shedex develops the solution and venture model, tests its technical and commercial feasibility, and prepares it for practical operation. Once validated, the venture can be handed over to economically vulnerable groups who can operate, own, and grow it as a source of livelihood and employment.

This approach brings together the capabilities required to build viable ventures, including innovation, technology development, manufacturing, finance, market access, partnerships, and capacity development. Shedex works to ensure that solutions are not only technically possible but also affordable, practical, market-oriented, and suitable for the people who will ultimately operate them.

Rather than focusing on isolated ideas or supporting startups that have already emerged, Shedex builds ventures around real opportunities and real community needs. Its work connects innovators, technical experts, manufacturers, investors, research institutions, government, development partners, markets, and communities where collaboration is necessary to bring a venture to life.

For Shedex, a venture is not necessarily the starting point—it can be the result of a deliberate process of identifying a need, developing a solution, testing it, and preparing it for ownership and operation by people who need economic opportunities.

By building and transferring practical ventures, Shedex aims to create pathways for economically vulnerable youth, women, unemployed and underemployed people, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other underserved groups to access livelihoods, generate income, create employment, and participate more fully in the economy.

The Challenges We Experienced

Our journey exposed structural obstacles that prevent many Ethiopian innovators from progressing beyond the idea stage. These include:

Limited access to affordable prototyping, fabrication, and manufacturing facilities
Shortage of engineering components, testing equipment, and specialized materials
High costs of importing research and development materials and equipment
Limited financing for prototype development and technology validation
Bureaucratic administrative procedures and fragmented institutional support
Premature business registration requirements before ideas are commercially ready
Monthly tax reporting obligations despite having no business revenue
Limited access to innovation workspaces, laboratories, and fabrication facilities
Weak coordination among institutions responsible for supporting innovation
Limited commercialization support for locally developed technologies

These barriers discourage innovators long before investors, incubators, or accelerators become involved.

How Shedex Reduces Innovation Risks

Shedex recognizes that turning an idea or social challenge into a viable venture involves technical, financial, manufacturing, market, institutional, and operational risks. These risks can be particularly difficult for economically vulnerable groups who may lack access to capital, technical expertise, facilities, networks, and market opportunities.

Shedex reduces these risks by taking responsibility for the early and more complex stages of venture development. Rather than expecting economically vulnerable groups to develop an idea, raise capital, build a product, test a market, and establish a business on their own, Shedex develops and validates the venture before it is handed over.

Shedex follows a build, pilot, and handover approach:

  • Identify a real social or economic challenge and an opportunity to address it.
  • Develop an affordable and practical solution suited to the target community and market.
  • Design the solution as a viable venture, including its operational, financial, production, and market model.
  • Mobilize the technical expertise, facilities, partnerships, financing, and other resources required for development.
  • Build prototypes, products, services, or initial venture models and test their technical and operational feasibility.
  • Pilot the venture in real-world conditions with potential users and beneficiaries.
  • Learn from the pilot, improve the solution, and validate its livelihood and market potential.
  • Establish a practical venture model that can be operated by the intended beneficiaries.
  • Handover the validated venture to economically vulnerable groups and support them with the knowledge, connections, and capabilities required to operate and grow it.

Shedex does not attempt to own every facility, technology, or service required to build a venture. Instead, it works as a venture-building orchestrator, connecting the capabilities of universities, TVET institutions, research centers, manufacturers, technology providers, financial institutions, investors, government institutions, development organizations, and market partners where they are needed.

Through this approach, Shedex helps reduce the risks that beneficiaries would otherwise face alone, including the risk of investing in untested ideas, acquiring inappropriate technologies, entering markets without validation, or attempting to build ventures without adequate technical and business support.

The objective is not simply to transfer a product or provide training. Shedex aims to transfer a tested and practical venture opportunity that people can operate, own, and develop into a sustainable source of income and employment.

This approach also allows Shedex to learn from each pilot and continuously improve its venture-building model. Successful solutions can be refined, replicated, adapted to different communities, and developed into additional opportunities for inclusive economic participation.

At Shedex, risk reduction means taking on the difficult work of building and testing opportunities before asking economically vulnerable people to take them forward. We build the venture, test the model, learn what works, and create a stronger foundation for people to take ownership and build sustainable livelihoods.

Shedex's Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Model (ILVM)

Shedex's Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Model (ILVM) is an integrated social innovation approach for developing practical solutions into viable ventures that create livelihoods, employment, and inclusive economic opportunities.

Unlike traditional incubators and accelerators that primarily support entrepreneurs after a venture has already been established, Shedex begins with the problem and the opportunity. It identifies real social and economic challenges, develops affordable and practical solutions, builds venture models around those solutions, tests them through real-world pilots, and validates their technical, operational, market, and livelihood potential.

Once a venture model has demonstrated its potential, Shedex works to prepare and hand it over to economically vulnerable groups who can operate, own, and grow the venture. In this way, Shedex does not simply train people to create ventures; it builds ventures that can create opportunities for people.

The model connects innovation, research, technology development, manufacturing, finance, market access, partnerships, and capacity development around the venture-building process. Ecosystem partners contribute the resources, expertise, infrastructure, markets, and networks required to make ventures viable and scalable.

The Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Model is built around eight stages:

1
Identify Social Challenges & Market Opportunities — Identify unmet social and economic needs, livelihood challenges, local production gaps, and market opportunities where a practical venture could create meaningful value.
2
Research, Innovation & Solution Development — Research the identified challenge and develop affordable, practical products, technologies, services, or other solutions capable of addressing it.
3
Prototype, Pilot & Validate — Build prototypes or initial venture models, test them in real-world settings, gather user and market feedback, and assess their technical, operational, commercial, and livelihood viability.
4
Build the Venture Model — Transform validated solutions into functioning venture models by defining the value proposition, target users, operating model, production or service process, revenue model, resource requirements, and pathways to sustainability.
5
Mobilize Resources & Partnerships — Connect the venture with appropriate finance, technical expertise, manufacturing capacity, research institutions, suppliers, markets, government bodies, private-sector partners, and other strategic resources needed for implementation.
6
Prepare & Handover the Venture — Prepare economically vulnerable groups with the knowledge, tools, systems, assets, partnerships, and practical capabilities required to operate the venture. Shedex then facilitates the transition of the validated venture to its intended beneficiaries for ownership, operation, and growth.
7
Livelihood & Employment Creation — Enable venture beneficiaries to generate income, create employment, develop productive skills, participate in markets, and build sustainable livelihoods through ventures designed around their economic realities.
8
Scale, Replicate & Expand Impact — Support successful venture models to be replicated, adapted, and expanded to reach more communities and beneficiaries, creating broader economic opportunities and contributing to inclusive and sustainable development.

The Shedex Difference

Shedex does not wait for economically vulnerable people to have an idea, capital, or established business before creating an opportunity for them.

Shedex identifies the opportunity, develops the solution, builds the venture, tests the model, and prepares it for handover—so that people can take ownership of a functioning opportunity and turn it into a livelihood.

Who We Are

Shedex is an Ethiopian Social Innovation Venture Studio dedicated to transforming ideas into sustainable enterprises and opportunities into lasting livelihoods.

We build and orchestrate innovation ecosystems that connect innovation, entrepreneurship, technology development, finance, manufacturing, market access, capacity building, and strategic partnerships to enable innovators and entrepreneurs to transform their ideas into enterprises that create decent jobs and generate lasting social, economic, and environmental impact.

Rather than waiting for startups to emerge, Shedex proactively identifies community livelihood challenges and untapped market opportunities, develops practical technologies and innovative solutions, validates them in real-world settings, mobilizes ecosystem partners and resources, supports commercialization and enterprise creation, and strengthens the ecosystems required for long-term growth and sustainability.

By bringing together government, universities, TVET institutions, research organizations, manufacturers, investors, development partners, the private sector, and local communities, Shedex creates collaborative ecosystems where innovation can flourish and solutions can be translated into scalable enterprises and inclusive economic opportunities.

Our mission is to empower innovators, entrepreneurs, youth, women, unemployed graduates, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other economically vulnerable groups to realize their dreams, build resilient enterprises, create decent jobs, and contribute to Ethiopia's inclusive and sustainable economic development.

Shedex does not simply support startups—we build the ecosystems that enable innovation to become enterprise, enterprise to create livelihoods, and livelihoods to transform communities.

Our Mission

To reduce poverty through innovation derisking, venture building, and the empowerment of economically vulnerable communities by building inclusive and accessible ecosystems that turn practical solutions into sustainable livelihood and employment opportunities.

We achieve this through four strategic missions:

1

Derisk Innovation

Identify promising ideas, technologies, and solutions that address real social and economic challenges, and reduce the technical, market, financial, operational, and commercialization risks that prevent them from becoming viable opportunities.

2

Build Practical Ventures

Develop affordable, practical solutions into functioning ventures through research, development, prototyping, testing, business model development, piloting, and market validation. Shedex builds ventures rather than simply supporting ideas or existing enterprises.

3

Empower Economically Vulnerable Communities

Build and validate ventures specifically designed to create economic opportunities for economically vulnerable youth, women, and other underserved communities. Shedex hands over tested ventures and supports beneficiaries with the knowledge, capabilities, tools, networks, and connections needed to own, operate, and grow them.

4

Build Inclusive and Accessible Ecosystems

Create ecosystems that make innovation, knowledge, technology, resources, partnerships, markets, finance, and venture-building opportunities more accessible to people and communities that are often excluded from conventional innovation and entrepreneurship systems.

Through these interconnected efforts, Shedex seeks to create practical pathways from innovation to income, from solutions to ventures, and from economic vulnerability to sustainable livelihoods—contributing ultimately to poverty reduction and more inclusive economic development.

Rather than asking, "How do we help more people start businesses?"
Shedex asks, "How do we identify real livelihood challenges, build practical and affordable ventures that address them, test those ventures in the real world, and place them in the hands of economically vulnerable people who can own, operate, and grow them?"

This philosophy defines Shedex's Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Model.

Innovation is the catalyst—not the destination.
Our ultimate goal is poverty reduction through practical venture creation that generates sustainable livelihoods, employment, and inclusive economic opportunities for economically vulnerable communities.

Who We Serve

Shedex exists to ensure that talent is not limited by opportunity. We build inclusive innovation ecosystems that enable individuals and communities to transform ideas into sustainable enterprises, create decent jobs, and achieve lasting livelihoods.

We recognize that economic vulnerability is not defined solely by income. It also includes people with talent, creativity, technical skills, education, or entrepreneurial ambition who lack access to finance, technology, manufacturing facilities, mentorship, markets, partnerships, or the institutional support needed to realize their potential.

Innovators, inventors, researchers, engineers, and technopreneurs developing solutions to real-world challenges
Youth and women seeking opportunities for entrepreneurship, employment, and economic empowerment
Unemployed TVET and university graduates, particularly engineering and technology graduates, who possess valuable skills but face barriers to productive employment and enterprise creation
Refugees, internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities striving to rebuild resilient and sustainable livelihoods
Persons with disabilities seeking accessible technologies, inclusive workplaces, entrepreneurship opportunities, and dignified employment
Smallholder farmers and rural communities requiring affordable technologies, climate-smart innovations, value-added processing, and improved market access to increase productivity and incomes
Micro, small, and growing enterprises seeking innovation, technology upgrading, manufacturing support, commercialization, and business growth opportunities
Informal sector workers and aspiring entrepreneurs transitioning toward productive, resilient, and sustainable enterprises
Low-income households and underserved communities that need affordable products, services, and technologies that improve quality of life and expand economic opportunity

Shedex also works closely with government institutions, universities, TVET institutions, research organizations, manufacturers, investors, financial institutions, development partners, private-sector organizations, cooperatives, and civil society to build collaborative ecosystems that enable innovation and inclusive economic development.

Every innovation, enterprise, partnership, and program we develop is intentionally designed to be inclusive. Whether creating physical technologies, digital platforms, manufacturing enterprises, or ecosystem initiatives, Shedex ensures that underserved populations have equitable access to innovation, technical skills, finance, production facilities, strategic partnerships, commercialization support, and market opportunities.

Unlike conventional innovation organizations that primarily support existing startups, Shedex proactively identifies untapped potential, removes systemic barriers, mobilizes ecosystem resources, and creates opportunities where they do not yet exist. Our goal is not simply to help people start businesses, but to empower them to become innovators, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, manufacturers, employers, and leaders who contribute to resilient local industries and inclusive economic growth.

By connecting people with the ecosystems they need to succeed, Shedex transforms ideas into sustainable enterprises, enterprises into decent jobs, and jobs into lasting livelihoods that strengthen families, communities, and Ethiopia's future.
