About Sigma

Discover why we were established, what makes us different, and how our Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Studio model transforms innovation into sustainable livelihoods across Ethiopia.

Why Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation Was Established

Most incubators and accelerators support entrepreneurs after they have already formed a startup, developed a prototype, or reached a minimum level of business readiness. However, in Ethiopia, many promising innovators never reach that stage — not because they lack ideas or talent, but because they face systemic barriers long before commercialization.

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation was established by founders who personally experienced these challenges while developing their own technologies and participating in an incubation program at Addis Ababa University. These firsthand experiences revealed significant gaps in Ethiopia's innovation ecosystem that existing support mechanisms rarely address.

Rather than creating another incubator or accelerator, the founders envisioned a new venture development model — one that removes barriers before they prevent innovation from succeeding and transforms innovative ideas into sustainable livelihood opportunities.

The Challenges We Experienced

Our journey exposed structural obstacles that prevent many Ethiopian innovators from progressing beyond the idea stage. These include:

Limited access to affordable prototyping, fabrication, and manufacturing facilities
Shortage of engineering components, testing equipment, and specialized materials
High costs of importing research and development materials and equipment
Limited financing for prototype development and technology validation
Bureaucratic administrative procedures and fragmented institutional support
Premature business registration requirements before ideas are commercially ready
Monthly tax reporting obligations despite having no business revenue
Limited access to innovation workspaces, laboratories, and fabrication facilities
Weak coordination among institutions responsible for supporting innovation
Limited commercialization support for locally developed technologies

These barriers discourage innovators long before investors, incubators, or accelerators become involved.

Why Sigma Is Different

Sigma was not established to compete with traditional incubators, accelerators, or venture studios. It was established to solve the challenges that occur before incubation begins and to create opportunities where none previously existed.

Instead of waiting for entrepreneurs to arrive with mature startups, Sigma proactively identifies livelihood challenges and unmet market opportunities, develops innovative solutions, validates them in real-world settings, establishes pilot enterprises, and transfers proven enterprise models to entrepreneurs and communities for sustainable implementation.

The enterprise is therefore the outcome of innovation — not the starting point.

How Sigma Reduces Innovation Risks

Sigma recognizes that no single organization can remove every barrier to innovation on its own. Instead, it serves as an Innovation Ecosystem Orchestrator, reducing the risks that individual innovators should not have to face alone by combining its own capabilities with those of strategic partners.

Depending on the needs of each innovation, Sigma facilitates access to:

  • Shared innovation, prototyping, and fabrication facilities
  • Engineering design, research, and product development expertise
  • Standard engineering components, manufacturing technologies, and production resources
  • Technical mentoring and innovation management support
  • Regulatory guidance, administrative facilitation, and institutional linkages
  • Government agencies, universities, research institutions, manufacturers, investors, financial institutions, and development partners
  • Pilot testing, market validation, and commercialization pathways
  • Venture creation, incubation, and enterprise development after innovations have demonstrated technical and commercial viability

Rather than attempting to own every resource, Sigma coordinates and mobilizes the capabilities of the broader innovation ecosystem, enabling innovators to focus on solving real-world problems while reducing technical, institutional, financial, and commercialization risks.

A New Venture Development Model

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation pioneers the Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Studio (ILVS) — a new venture development model that differs fundamentally from traditional incubators, accelerators, and venture studios.

Traditional support organizations primarily assist entrepreneurs and startups that already exist. Sigma begins much earlier by identifying community livelihood challenges and market opportunities, developing innovative solutions, validating them with users, establishing pilot enterprises, and transferring successful business models to entrepreneurs and communities for large-scale implementation.

Its approach transforms innovation into sustainable livelihoods, productive enterprises, and inclusive economic growth.

Our Philosophy

Innovation should not fail because a technopreneur cannot find a workshop, import a sensor, obtain a support letter, secure engineering components, or comply with unnecessary administrative requirements before earning their first income.

Likewise, communities should not continue struggling with livelihood challenges simply because practical solutions have not been developed or commercialized.

Sigma exists to remove these barriers by developing innovative solutions, building sustainable enterprises, and creating inclusive economic opportunities that improve lives, generate employment, and promote environmentally responsible development.

Who We Are

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation is an Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Studio, Technology Development Organization, Innovation Ecosystem Orchestrator, and Enterprise Builder.

We develop innovative solutions that address community livelihood challenges, validate them through practical implementation, transform them into sustainable enterprises, and transfer proven models to entrepreneurs and communities.

Unlike traditional incubators that primarily support existing startups, Sigma creates opportunities where none previously existed by bridging the gap between innovation, enterprise creation, and livelihood transformation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop innovative solutions that solve community livelihood challenges, create sustainable jobs, improve productivity, simplify people's daily lives, strengthen local industries, and promote environmentally and climate-friendly development.

We achieve this through four strategic missions:

1

De-risk Early-Stage Innovation

Partner with innovators, inventors, researchers, engineers, technopreneurs, and communities whose ideas are too early-stage for traditional incubators, accelerators, or investors. Sigma shares the technical, commercial, operational, and ecosystem risks of innovation by facilitating access to research, engineering expertise, prototyping, validation, commercialization support, and strategic partnerships — transforming promising ideas into investment-ready and commercially viable enterprises.

2

Build Innovative Solutions that Improve Lives and Create Jobs

Develop practical technologies, products, services, business models, and other innovative solutions that solve real-world challenges, improve productivity, simplify everyday life, substitute imports where feasible, and create sustainable employment opportunities. Sigma establishes innovation spaces where engineering graduates, TVET graduates, youth, women, refugees, persons with disabilities, and other underserved groups can develop practical skills, create innovations, and build sustainable enterprises.

3

Transform Market Gaps into Sustainable Enterprises

Identify unmet market needs and convert them into scalable business opportunities by designing, testing, validating, and demonstrating innovative enterprise models. Sigma establishes pilot ventures and transfers proven business models to youth, women, refugees, cooperatives, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups through partnerships with government institutions, development partners, financial institutions, academia, and the private sector.

4

Build an Inclusive Innovation Ecosystem

Coordinate and mobilize the broader innovation ecosystem by connecting innovators with shared facilities, engineering expertise, institutional support, market linkages, and commercialization pathways. Sigma serves as an ecosystem orchestrator that reduces barriers and creates enabling conditions for innovation to flourish across Ethiopia.

Rather than asking, "How do we build more startups?"
Sigma asks, "How do we solve livelihood challenges through innovation and transform those solutions into sustainable enterprises that improve people's lives?"

This philosophy defines Sigma's Innovation-to-Livelihood Venture Studio model.

Innovation is not our final product.
Sustainable livelihoods, inclusive enterprises, and improved quality of life are.

Who We Serve

Sigma EcoBuild & Innovation develops innovative enterprises and technologies that create sustainable livelihood opportunities for underserved and vulnerable communities across Ethiopia. We recognize that vulnerability extends beyond poverty — it also includes individuals with skills, education, or resources who lack access to meaningful economic opportunities.

Our Primary Target Groups

Youth and women facing unemployment or underemployment
Refugees, internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host communities
Smallholder farmers seeking affordable technologies that improve productivity, reduce labor, and increase incomes
TVET graduates and engineering graduates who possess technical skills but lack employment opportunities, access to equipment, finance, and markets
Informal sector workers who require improved technologies, skills, and business models to transition into sustainable enterprises
Persons with disabilities requiring accessible technologies and inclusive livelihood opportunities
Micro and small enterprises seeking innovative products, manufacturing technologies, and business growth support
Low-income households and communities lacking access to affordable products and services that improve quality of life

Sigma intentionally designs every innovation, enterprise, and program to be inclusive. Whether developing physical technologies, digital solutions, or green manufacturing enterprises, we ensure that underserved populations have equitable access to innovation, technical training, production facilities, finance, market linkages, and long-term business development support.

Unlike conventional innovation organizations that primarily serve existing entrepreneurs or startups, Sigma proactively identifies vulnerable groups with untapped potential, develops solutions tailored to their livelihood challenges, and transforms them into productive entrepreneurs, skilled workers, innovators, manufacturers, and enterprise owners.

Through this Innovation-to-Livelihood approach, we enable individuals and communities to build sustainable businesses, create decent jobs, increase household incomes, and contribute to Ethiopia's inclusive and climate-resilient economic transformation.
