About Shedex

Building Innovation That Creates Jobs and Changes Lives

Shedex EcoBuild & Innovation is an Ethiopian Social Innovation Venture Studio dedicated to transforming ideas into sustainable enterprises and opportunities into lasting livelihoods. Rather than focusing on innovation alone, Shedex builds complete ecosystems that connect innovators with entrepreneurship, technology, finance, manufacturing, market access, strategic partnerships, and capacity-building support—enabling ideas to grow into impactful businesses. At the heart of Shedex's approach is the belief that innovation should solve real-world challenges while creating decent employment and inclusive economic opportunities. Instead of waiting for startups to emerge, Shedex proactively identifies unmet market needs, develops practical technologies, validates business models, mobilizes resources, and builds scalable enterprises capable of generating measurable social, economic, and environmental impact. Shedex serves as a bridge between innovation and implementation, empowering innovators, entrepreneurs, youth, women, unemployed graduates, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other economically vulnerable groups to transform their ideas into successful enterprises. Through venture creation, innovation commercialization, crowdfunding, incubation, enterprise development, manufacturing support, and ecosystem partnerships, Shedex helps individuals realize their dreams while strengthening local industries and contributing to Ethiopia's sustainable and inclusive economic development. By building innovation ecosystems rather than isolated projects, Shedex creates lasting pathways for job creation, productivity growth, poverty reduction, and resilient community development—ensuring that every promising idea has the opportunity to become a sustainable enterprise that changes lives.
