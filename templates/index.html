About Shedex

Building Ventures That Create Jobs and Change Lives

Shedex is a social innovation venture studio dedicated to developing practical, affordable ventures that create livelihoods, employment, and inclusive economic opportunities for economically vulnerable youth, women, and other underserved communities.

Rather than simply supporting existing enterprises or focusing on innovation for its own sake, Shedex builds ventures from the ground up. It identifies real social and economic challenges, develops practical solutions, designs viable venture models, and tests them through pilots in real-world settings.

Shedex follows a build, pilot, and handover approach. We develop and validate solutions, demonstrate their practicality and sustainability through pilot ventures, and then hand them over to economically vulnerable groups who can operate, own, and grow them as sources of livelihood and employment. This approach enables communities to access not only ideas or training, but functioning venture models that are designed around their economic realities.

Shedex connects innovation, venture building, technology, manufacturing, finance, market access, partnerships, and capacity development to transform practical solutions into viable ventures. Each venture is designed to address a genuine need while creating opportunities for people who have limited access to employment, capital, markets, and productive assets.

Our work focuses particularly on economically vulnerable youth, women, unemployed and underemployed people, refugees, persons with disabilities, smallholder farmers, and other underserved communities. By building ventures that are affordable, practical, and adaptable to local conditions, Shedex creates pathways for these groups to become venture owners, producers, service providers, and income-generating participants in the economy.

Shedex therefore goes beyond developing products or projects. We build ventures with the potential to create sustainable livelihoods, decent employment, local production, and inclusive economic participation. Through this model, Shedex seeks to turn real-world challenges into practical opportunities and promising solutions into ventures that can change lives.
