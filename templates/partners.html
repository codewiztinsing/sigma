Why Partner With Shedex?

Shedex serves as an Innovation Ecosystem Orchestrator — we don't try to do everything alone. Instead, we coordinate and mobilize the capabilities of diverse partners to create outcomes no single organization could achieve independently.

What Partners Gain

  • Access to Innovations: Early access to technologies and enterprises developed through our programs.
  • Pipeline of Talent: Connection to trained youth, engineers, and entrepreneurs ready for employment or investment.
  • Market Insights: Deep understanding of community needs, market gaps, and opportunities across Ethiopia.
  • Shared Impact: Measurable outcomes in job creation, enterprise development, and inclusive growth.
  • Network Expansion: Connection to a broad ecosystem of innovators, institutions, and development actors.
