What We Do

Our three integrated venture-building pathways transform market challenges into sustainable enterprises that create jobs and improve livelihoods across Ethiopia.

Pathway 1: Opportunity-Driven Innovation and Venture Creation

Shedex proactively identifies community livelihood challenges, unmet market needs, and emerging economic opportunities before entrepreneurs or startups exist. Rather than waiting for solutions to appear, we initiate the innovation process by transforming challenges into practical technologies, venture models, and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

How It Works

  • Opportunity Discovery: We systematically identify pressing livelihood challenges, underserved markets, and opportunities for innovation across Ethiopia's priority sectors.
  • Innovation & Solution Development: We develop practical technologies, products, services, digital platforms, and venture models tailored to solve identified challenges.
  • Validation & Ecosystem Mobilization: Solutions are tested with end users while Shedex mobilizes the ecosystem—bringing together researchers, engineers, manufacturers, financiers, government institutions, universities, development partners, and private-sector collaborators to strengthen the innovation.
  • Pilot Venture & Commercialization: Validated innovations are demonstrated through pilot ventures, manufacturing partnerships, or commercialization initiatives to prove technical, commercial, and social viability.
  • Venture Transfer, Scaling & Ecosystem Expansion: Successful venture models are expanded through selected economically vulnerable, entrepreneur-minded communities, cooperatives, community organizations, private-sector partners, and public institutions, creating sustainable ventures, decent jobs, and lasting livelihood opportunities.

Through this pathway, Shedex ensures that promising opportunities are not lost simply because no entrepreneur has yet emerged. We create the conditions that allow opportunities to become innovations, innovations to become ventures, and ventures to generate inclusive economic impact. The pathway is particularly designed to reach economically vulnerable communities with entrepreneurial potential and enable selected participants to become owners, operators, or participants in validated ventures.

Pathway 2: Innovation and Venture Co-Building

Shedex partners with innovators, inventors, researchers, engineers, technopreneurs, and early-stage entrepreneurs whose innovations are at very early stages and require technical, financial, institutional, manufacturing, and market support before they can become sustainable ventures.

This pathway is specifically designed for innovators who are often excluded from conventional innovation ecosystems because their innovations are still at an early stage, they may not yet have a prototype or minimum viable product (MVP), or they are geographically distant from conventional urban incubators and accelerators. Shedex provides an accessible pathway through which promising innovations can begin their development journey without requiring innovators to first meet conventional ecosystem entry requirements.

Rather than acting solely as an advisor or incubator, Shedex becomes a long-term ecosystem partner—reducing risks, mobilizing resources, and connecting innovators with the capabilities needed to transform early-stage ideas and innovations into real-world impact.

Our Support Includes

  • Innovation & Technology Development: Engineering design, research and development, product improvement, and technical problem-solving.
  • Prototyping, Testing & Validation: Access to shared innovation spaces, fabrication facilities, testing equipment, and user validation.
  • Business & Commercialization Support: Business model development, customer discovery, commercialization strategies, and market validation.
  • Manufacturing & Production Support: Access to production facilities, engineering expertise, technology transfer, and manufacturing partnerships.
  • Finance & Investment Facilitation: Crowdfunding, grant opportunities, investor readiness, financial institutions, and venture financing mechanisms.
  • Strategic Partnerships & Market Access: Connections with government, universities, TVET institutions, research organizations, manufacturers, development partners, suppliers, distributors, and customers.
  • Venture Development & Growth: Legal and operational support, governance, business systems, scaling strategies, and long-term ecosystem integration.

Shedex works alongside innovators as a collaborative ecosystem partner, sharing knowledge, networks, opportunities, and appropriate risks throughout the innovation journey. Our goal is not simply to help innovators launch startups, but to build resilient ventures that create decent jobs, strengthen local industries, solve real-world challenges, and improve lives.

Pathway 3: Technology Innovation for Decent Jobs and Real-World Solutions

Shedex harnesses technology and innovation to solve real-world challenges while creating decent jobs, sustainable ventures, and lasting livelihood opportunities. Rather than developing technologies as standalone products, Shedex builds the ecosystems that enable innovations to progress from ideas to prototypes, from prototypes to ventures, and from ventures to long-term social, economic, and environmental impact.

Through this pathway, Shedex identifies pressing community needs and market opportunities, develops practical technologies, digital platforms, engineering solutions, and manufacturing innovations, validates them in real-world settings, and connects them with finance, manufacturing, commercialization, strategic partnerships, and market access. By integrating every stage of the innovation journey, Shedex ensures that technology becomes a catalyst for venture creation, industrial development, and inclusive economic growth.

Our ecosystem-driven approach creates meaningful opportunities for innovators, researchers, engineers, TVET graduates, university graduates, youth, women, refugees, persons with disabilities, and other economically vulnerable groups to acquire practical skills, participate in technology development, gain hands-on industry experience, launch ventures, and build sustainable careers. Every innovation is intentionally designed to create value not only for end users but also for the people involved in developing, manufacturing, commercializing, and scaling it.

The technologies and ventures emerging from this pathway improve productivity and efficiency, expand access to affordable products and services, strengthen local manufacturing, promote import substitution, accelerate digital transformation, support climate-smart and environmentally responsible development, and improve livelihoods across Ethiopia. From AI-powered digital platforms and intelligent automation systems to engineering equipment, renewable energy technologies, sustainable construction solutions, advanced manufacturing, and other market-driven innovations, Shedex transforms technology into opportunities for people and communities.

Our success is measured not only by the technologies we develop, but by the ecosystems we strengthen, the ventures we build, the decent jobs we create, the skills we develop, the industries we grow, and the lives we improve.

Our Services

Research & Innovation is the foundation of everything we do. We conduct applied research to understand community livelihood challenges, market gaps, and technological opportunities. Our research informs the design of practical solutions that are technically feasible, economically viable, and socially impactful.

  • Community needs assessment and livelihood analysis
  • Market research and opportunity identification
  • Technology scouting and adaptation
  • Feasibility studies and proof-of-concept design

Prototyping transforms ideas into tangible products. We provide access to fabrication facilities, engineering expertise, and testing environments where innovations can be built, refined, and validated before commercialization.

  • Product design and engineering
  • 3D modeling and simulation
  • Prototype fabrication and assembly
  • Field testing and user validation

Enterprise Building turns validated innovations into sustainable businesses. We design business models, establish pilot ventures, and transfer proven enterprises to entrepreneurs and communities.

Our services include:

  • Enterprise and business model development
  • Commercialization strategy
  • Manufacturing and production support
  • Crowdfunding and investment readiness
  • Market access and business development
  • Technology transfer and licensing support
  • Incubation, venture building, and enterprise growth
  • Strategic partnership development

Innovation Ecosystem Building & Capacity Development

As a Social Innovation Venture Studio, Shedex connects innovators with the institutions, knowledge, infrastructure, finance, and partnerships needed to succeed. We strengthen collaboration across government, academia, industry, investors, and development partners to create enabling environments for innovation and inclusive economic development.

Our services include:

  • Innovation ecosystem design and coordination
  • Partnership development and stakeholder engagement
  • Capacity building and technical training
  • Innovation and entrepreneurship programs
  • Mentorship and coaching
  • Institutional strengthening
  • Innovation policy and ecosystem advisory
  • Multi-sector collaboration and network facilitation
