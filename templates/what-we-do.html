What We Do

Our three integrated venture-building pathways transform market challenges into sustainable enterprises that create jobs and improve livelihoods across Ethiopia.

Pathway 1: Proactive Opportunity-Driven Enterprise Creation

We identify unmet market needs before startups exist, then design, validate, and launch enterprises to serve them. This pathway addresses gaps where no entrepreneur has yet emerged to solve a pressing community or market challenge.

How It Works

  • Market Scanning: We systematically identify unmet needs, underserved communities, and market gaps across Ethiopia's key sectors.
  • Solution Design: Our team designs innovative technologies, products, services, and business models tailored to each identified opportunity.
  • Validation: We test solutions with real users in real-world settings, iterating based on feedback and data.
  • Pilot Enterprise: We establish pilot ventures to demonstrate viability and generate proof-of-concept.
  • Transfer: Proven enterprise models are transferred to entrepreneurs, cooperatives, or communities for sustainable scaling.

This pathway ensures that no promising opportunity goes unexplored simply because an entrepreneur has not yet identified it.

Pathway 2: Venture Building for Early-Stage Technopreneurs

We co-build with founders and innovators who have promising ideas but lack the structure, resources, or market linkages needed to transform concepts into viable enterprises.

Our Support Includes

  • Technical Mentoring: Engineering, design, and product development guidance from experienced professionals.
  • Prototyping Access: Shared fabrication facilities, tools, and equipment for building and testing.
  • Business Model Validation: Market research, customer discovery, and revenue model testing.
  • Market Linkages: Connections to buyers, suppliers, distributors, and strategic partners.
  • Operational Structure: Legal, administrative, and operational frameworks for sustainable growth.
  • Funding Facilitation: Preparation for investment readiness and introductions to potential funders.

We work alongside innovators as venture-building partners, not just advisors — sharing risks, resources, and rewards.

Pathway 3: Technology Innovation, Industrialization & Enterprise Development

We develop and industrialize practical technologies that strengthen local manufacturing, increase productivity, and create engineering-based enterprises.

Focus Areas

  • Green Technologies: Sustainable solutions for energy, water, waste, and environmental challenges.
  • Agro-processing Equipment: Affordable machinery that reduces post-harvest losses and increases farmer incomes.
  • Renewable Energy Systems: Solar, biogas, and other clean energy technologies tailored to local conditions.
  • Sustainable Building Materials: Eco-friendly construction technologies and materials.
  • Digital Innovation: Software, IoT, and data-driven solutions for agriculture, health, and commerce.
  • Circular Economy: Waste-to-value technologies and recycling enterprises.

Each technology is designed for local manufacturing, using locally available materials where possible, to ensure affordability, maintainability, and scalability.

Our Services

Research & Innovation is the foundation of everything we do. We conduct applied research to understand community livelihood challenges, market gaps, and technological opportunities. Our research informs the design of practical solutions that are technically feasible, economically viable, and socially impactful.

  • Community needs assessment and livelihood analysis
  • Market research and opportunity identification
  • Technology scouting and adaptation
  • Feasibility studies and proof-of-concept design

Prototyping transforms ideas into tangible products. We provide access to fabrication facilities, engineering expertise, and testing environments where innovations can be built, refined, and validated before commercialization.

  • Product design and engineering
  • 3D modeling and simulation
  • Prototype fabrication and assembly
  • Field testing and user validation

Enterprise Building turns validated innovations into sustainable businesses. We design business models, establish pilot ventures, and transfer proven enterprises to entrepreneurs and communities.

  • Business model design and validation
  • Pilot enterprise establishment
  • Entrepreneur training and mentorship
  • Technology transfer and licensing

Ecosystem Support connects innovators with the broader network of resources they need to succeed. We orchestrate partnerships, facilitate access to facilities, and advocate for policies that enable innovation.

  • Partnership development and coordination
  • Access to shared facilities and equipment
  • Regulatory and administrative support
  • Market linkages and commercialization pathways
